## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an archival, cinematic, and solemn editorial tribute experience crafted for milestone retrospectives and sports documentary features. The visual tone invokes the weight of history, prestige, and athletic mythology seen in prestige documentary works by HBO Sports and A24. 

Key attributes:
- **Atmospheric Grandeur:** Monolithic dark surfaces anchored by absolute pitch blacks, weathered titanium, and sparingly deployed antique saffron gold accents.
- **Solemnity & Prestige:** The interface avoids playful, tech-startup tropes. It treats historical statistics, career chapters, and audio-visual archives like sacred museum artifacts.
- **High-Contrast Minimalism:** Visual weight is achieved through monumental scale contrasts—pairing towering condensed display headers with meticulous, wide-tracked micro-typography.
- **Zero Gimmicks:** Strictly no frosted glassmorphism, no rounded playful pills, no fluorescent neon glows, and no decorative gradients. Surfaces are dry, tangible, disciplined, and framed by whisper-thin graphite boundaries.

## Colors

The palette operates under intense restraint. The dark environment is calibrated to create immense depth without muddy grayness, while gold is reserved strictly for milestones, honors, active documentary chapter indicators, and archival accents.

### Color Roles
- **Base Canvas (`#080808`):** Deep obsidian void. Used for global backdrops and cinematic framing.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#0d0d0d`):** Primary card and section container background.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#141414`):** Elevated narrative blocks, video overlay cards, and player asset modules.
- **Borders & Rules (`#262626`):** Low-contrast graphite stroke providing architectural structural discipline without breaking immersion.
- **Primary / Antique Gold (`#d4af37`):** The hallmark accent. Used with surgical discretion for match milestones, world records, trophy badges, and active scrubber states. Secondary deeper gold (`#9b781e`) handles subtle hover states and low-key accents.
- **Titanium Primary Text (`#e2e2e5`):** Crisp weathered silver offering stark, uncompromised legibility for high-impact typography.
- **Titanium Muted (`#9ca3af`):** Body copy, documentary narration transcripts, and structural framing labels.

## Typography

Typography delivers the tension between visceral, monumental sports drama and calculated documentary archival precision.

- **Monumental Display (`Bebas Neue`):** All major headers, run counts, inning totals, and era markers are set in tall, condensed display lettering. Titles always render in uppercase, radiating athletic scale.
- **Narrative Prose (`Source Serif 4`):** Long-form journalism, chapter synopses, and oral quotes use a refined, literary serif that introduces quiet introspection and prestige publishing cadence.
- **Archival Data & Metadata (`JetBrains Mono`):** Match dates, stadium coordinates, strike rates, overs bowled, and documentary runtime metadata use tracked monospaced characters in all-caps to evoke precision record-keeping and tape archives.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an architectural 12-column editorial grid for desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single-column rhythm on mobile devices.

### Layout Principles
- **Editorial Breathers:** Major milestone numbers and archival imagery are given vast negative space (`space-xxl`) to replicate art gallery installations and double-page photographic spreads.
- **Split-Screen Ratio:** Key narrative sequences use an asymmetric 7:5 ratio, where 7 columns are dedicated to full-bleed documentary frame captures, and 5 columns hold archival statistics, transcripts, and scorebooks.
- **Grid Discipline:** Content boundaries adhere strictly to hairline dividers. Elements align squarely to column edges; off-grid floating decorative elements are prohibited.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately rejects drop shadows and translucent blur overlays. Depth is rendered via tangible luminance stepping and disciplined physical borders:

- **Tonal Stepping:** Surfaces elevate by shifting from `#080808` to `#0d0d0d` and `#141414`. Higher surfaces represent foreground chronological events, scorecard details, and interactive media drawers.
- **Hairline Framing:** Depth and separation between containers are delineated solely by 1px solid graphite rules (`#262626`).
- **Luminance Masks:** Cinematic imagery bleeds out into `#080808` using sharp linear edge masks or subtle grainy dark vignettes rather than floating on drop-shadowed cards.
- **Active State Highlights:** Selected items or highlighted career phases establish dominance through a razor-thin 1px gold (`#d4af37`) edge or border accent, never a glowing diffusion.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly sharp (`roundedness: 0`). 

- Every container, button, image frame, video viewport, and chip features crisp 90-degree 0px corners.
- Rectilinear purity reinforces the gravitas of monument steles, black-and-white film strip edges, archival plaques, and classic broadsheet cricket scorecards.
- Rounded corners or pill buttons are strictly forbidden across all touchpoints.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Links
- **Primary Archival Action:** Solid `#d4af37` background, pitch `#080808` text set in `JetBrains Mono` bold uppercase with `0.15em` tracking. 0px border-radius, strict rectangular geometry. On hover, background shifts to antique brass (`#c59b27`).
- **Secondary Frame Action:** Transparent canvas, 1px solid border in `#262626`, titanium text (`#e2e2e5`). On hover, the border turns `#d4af37` and the label color lifts to pure white.
- **Play/Reel Trigger:** Square button containing a sharp directional icon flanked by mono timecode (`00:00 / RECORDED ARCHIVE`).

### Cards & Artifact Modules
- **Milestone Cards:** Background `#0d0d0d` with a 1px border (`#262626`). Top header features a micro mono tag (`MATCH 412 // MELBOURNE`) followed by a monumental Bebas Neue statistic (`183*`) and two lines of Source Serif narrative.
- **Hover/Interaction:** Card border shifts from `#262626` to `#d4af37` with an immediate zero-friction transition; no mechanical bounce or easing elevation.

### Data Chips & Timeline Badges
- **Format:** Fully rectangular bounding boxes with 1px hairline border (`#262626`) and background `#141414`.
- **Text:** `label-caps` in `#9ca3af`. For special accolades (e.g., `WORLD CUP '11`, `MAN OF THE MATCH`), border and text transition to `#d4af37`.

### Lists & Statistical Tables
- **Scorecard Rows:** Minimal row height with strict 1px bottom border (`#1a1a1a`). Inning stats align right using tabular figures from `JetBrains Mono`. Opponent and venue names render in `Source Serif 4`.
- **Career Phase Chapters:** Numbered serial layout (`01`, `02`, `03`) in muted antique gold, accompanied by condensed chapter headers and brief editorial quotes.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Search & Filter Bars:** Flat rectangular fields with `#0d0d0d` interior, 1px `#262626` outline, placeholder text in muted titanium (`#9ca3af`), and active focus state indicated by a razor 1px `#d4af37` perimeter line. No glowing focus rings.

### Checkboxes & Segment Controls
- **Checkboxes:** Exact 14px square boxes, `#080808` background, 1px border (`#262626`). Checked state fills with `#d4af37` and features an obsidian mark.
- **Timeline Segment Control:** Monolithic joined bar segments divided by 1px vertical graphite lines. Active segment marked by a gold underline bar (1px height) along the bottom perimeter.
